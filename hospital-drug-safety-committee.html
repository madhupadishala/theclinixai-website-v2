Hospital Drug-Safety Committee

Drug-safety oversight benefits from multidisciplinary review across medicine, pharmacy, nursing, quality and administration. The committee should use reliable evidence, distinguish individual-case action from system learning and track decisions through verified completion.

01

Mandate

Define scope, membership, quorum, escalation, confidentiality and decision rights.

02

Evidence review

Evaluate ADRs, medication errors, trends, high-risk medicines and emerging concerns.

03

Accountable action

Assign owners, due dates, evidence requirements and effectiveness review.

CONTROL FRAMEWORK

Recognise. Report. Review. Learn. Prevent.

  1. Make reporting accessible to relevant staff and patients.
  2. Preserve clinical evidence and protect information appropriately.
  3. Apply accountable assessment and escalation.
  4. Identify patterns without overstating incident-reporting data.
  5. Track action and effectiveness through governance.
