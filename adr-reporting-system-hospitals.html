ADR Reporting System for Hospitals

An ADR reporting system should reduce reporting friction while collecting enough evidence for meaningful follow-up. It must distinguish initial notification from formal assessment and provide feedback so clinical teams understand that reporting produces action.

01

Accessible intake

Offer clear reporting routes appropriate to hospital roles and patient access.

02

Clinical evidence

Capture patient, medicine, reaction, timing, investigations, outcome and reporter context.

03

Controlled follow-up

Prioritise missing information and preserve all communication and assessment evidence.

CONTROL FRAMEWORK

Recognise. Report. Review. Learn. Prevent.

  1. Make reporting accessible to relevant staff and patients.
  2. Preserve clinical evidence and protect information appropriately.
  3. Apply accountable assessment and escalation.
  4. Identify patterns without overstating incident-reporting data.
  5. Track action and effectiveness through governance.

AUTHORISED CONTEXT
