1. Scope and responsibility

This privacy policy explains how TheClinixAI (“ClinixAI”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles personal information when you visit theclinixai.com, read our scientific resources, contact us, request a demonstration, enquire about services or academy programmes, or otherwise interact with our website.

For personal information collected through this website, TheClinixAI is the organisation responsible for deciding why and how that information is used. Questions or privacy requests may be sent to support@theclinixai.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

When you submit an enquiry or registration, we may receive your name, professional email address, telephone or WhatsApp number, organisation, role, country, programme or service interests, and the message or other information you choose to provide.

Website and device information

Our hosting and security infrastructure may process technical information required to deliver and protect the website, such as IP address, request time, browser or device type, requested URL and security-related logs.

Analytics information—only with your permission

If you accept analytics, we may measure page views, referral source, campaign parameters, general engagement, scroll depth, downloads, outbound links and enquiry-journey events. We do not intentionally send the contents of contact forms, patient information or free-text messages to analytics.

3. How we use information

We use personal information only where it is relevant to a defined purpose, including to:

  • respond to enquiries and arrange discovery, product, service or academy conversations;
  • provide requested information, resources, programmes or services;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the website;
  • understand content and service interest when analytics consent is given;
  • maintain appropriate business, compliance and communication records; and
  • meet legal obligations and protect legitimate rights, systems and users.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use website analytics to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about an individual.

4. Legal bases and choices

Depending on your location and the interaction, we may rely on your consent, steps requested before entering a contract, performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, or our legitimate interests in operating a secure, relevant and accountable business website.

Optional analytics is based on your choice. It remains off until you accept it through our privacy banner. You can withdraw that choice at any time using the “Cookie settings” control; withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before it.

5. Service providers and international processing

We may use carefully selected providers that support website hosting, security, communications, analytics and business operations. They process information under their own terms or on our instructions, as applicable.

Some providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate contractual, organisational or legal safeguards for international transfers.

6. Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including enquiry management, delivery of requested services, security, record keeping and applicable legal obligations. Retention periods vary according to the nature of the information and the relationship.

We use proportionate technical and organisational measures designed to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so we continually review controls appropriate to the information and risk.

7. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of, deletion of or restriction on the use of your personal information; object to certain processing; withdraw consent; or request a portable copy. You may also complain to the relevant data-protection authority.

To make a request, email support@theclinixai.com. We may need to verify your identity and clarify the information involved before completing a request.

8. Important notice about safety and health data

The general website contact forms are not adverse-event reporting channels and are not designed to receive individual case safety reports, patient identifiers, medical records or confidential health information. Please do not submit such information through a general enquiry form.

If a dedicated safety-reporting route is made available, its purpose, required information and applicable privacy notice will be stated separately. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact local emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional.

9. Children

This website and our business services are intended for professionals and organisations, not children. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children through this website.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services, providers or legal obligations change. We will publish the updated version here and revise the effective or review date above.
