Adverse-Event Intake from Healthcare Professionals

Healthcare professionals may report through hospitals, medical information, manufacturers, partners or national systems. Intake should preserve the reporter's words, clinical context and receipt information while enabling appropriate follow-up and downstream routing.

01

Preserve

Retain original report content, attachments, timestamps and source identity controls.

02

Clarify

Request clinically meaningful missing information without leading the reporter.

03

Route

Direct information to hospital learning, pharmacovigilance or partner exchange as applicable.

CONTROL FRAMEWORK

Recognise. Report. Review. Learn. Prevent.

  1. Make reporting accessible to relevant staff and patients.
  2. Preserve clinical evidence and protect information appropriately.
  3. Apply accountable assessment and escalation.
  4. Identify patterns without overstating incident-reporting data.
  5. Track action and effectiveness through governance.

AUTHORISED CONTEXT

Designed around recognised patient-safety principles.

Hospitals must apply applicable national requirements and institutional procedures. Useful authoritative context includes WHO Medication Without Harm · WHO incident reporting guidance · WHO pharmacovigilance · PvPI ADR Monitoring Centres.
