THE OPERATIONAL RISK

Checklist completion does not guarantee case integrity.

A case can be technically complete and still be scientifically inconsistent: chronology may conflict with structured dates, seriousness may not match the clinical description, the narrative may omit decisive evidence, or the submission fields may differ from the approved assessment. Nexus Quality Workspace combines deterministic checks, evidence comparison and reviewer judgement.

CONTROLLED END-TO-END FLOW

One workflow. Five accountable decisions.

01

Compare

Reconcile source evidence against structured case fields and the narrative.

02

Challenge

Flag contradictions in dates, products, events, seriousness, assessments and reportability.

03

Prioritise

Rank findings by potential patient-safety, compliance and submission impact.

04

Resolve

Create attributable queries and preserve response, correction and approval history.

05

Release

Confirm critical controls and produce a defensible submission-readiness decision.

CAPABILITY ARCHITECTURE

Designed around the work safety teams must defend.

Automation handles repeatable structure. Qualified professionals retain scientific and regulatory authority.

Source-to-field verification

Highlight omissions and mismatches between evidence and structured data.

Cross-field consistency

Detect chronology, coding, seriousness and assessment contradictions.

Narrative reconciliation

Compare decisive source facts with the approved case narrative.

Risk-based QC

Apply defined review depth based on case type, complexity and compliance risk.

Query governance

Track finding ownership, response, correction, approval and recurrence.

Quality intelligence

Trend material errors by process, team, vendor, product and root-cause category.

GOVERNED AI

Assistance is visible. Accountability remains human.

ClinixAI is designed to show the evidence, rule, model output, confidence, reviewer action and final disposition as separate records. The system does not convert an AI suggestion into an invisible regulatory decision.

  • Defined intended use and controlled configuration
  • Tenant-specific knowledge and procedural context
  • Model, prompt, rule and knowledge versioning
  • Human override with attributable rationale
  • Performance monitoring and controlled change

REGULATORY CONTEXT

Built to support governed execution—not to replace regional requirements.

Configuration and validation must reflect the marketing authorisation holder’s products, procedures, territories and applicable requirements. Primary references for this solution include EMA GVP Module VI · ICH E2B(R3) specification.
