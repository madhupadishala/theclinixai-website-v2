Medication-Safety Audit and CAPA for Hospitals

Audits can evaluate medication governance, storage, prescribing, dispensing, administration, monitoring, incident reporting and learning. Findings should describe objective evidence and risk; CAPA should address causes rather than merely restating the observation.

01

Risk-based audit

Prioritise high-risk medicines, processes, settings, transitions and previous failures.

02

Root-cause discipline

Separate immediate containment, contributing factors and systemic causes.

03

Effectiveness

Define evidence and monitoring periods capable of showing whether risk was reduced.

CONTROL FRAMEWORK

Recognise. Report. Review. Learn. Prevent.

  1. Make reporting accessible to relevant staff and patients.
  2. Preserve clinical evidence and protect information appropriately.
  3. Apply accountable assessment and escalation.
  4. Identify patterns without overstating incident-reporting data.
  5. Track action and effectiveness through governance.
