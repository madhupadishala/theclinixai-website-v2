Patient-Safety Incident Reporting System

Incident systems are not incidence-rate databases and should not be interpreted without understanding underreporting and selection. Their strength is structured learning: identifying hazards, contributing factors, repeated patterns and opportunities to reduce avoidable harm.

01

Minimum information

Collect sufficient structured and narrative context without making reporting impractical.

02

Learning model

Classify harm, event type, contributing factors, detection and recovery.

03

Feedback loop

Communicate actions and learning so reporters can see the purpose of participation.

CONTROL FRAMEWORK

Recognise. Report. Review. Learn. Prevent.

  1. Make reporting accessible to relevant staff and patients.
  2. Preserve clinical evidence and protect information appropriately.
  3. Apply accountable assessment and escalation.
  4. Identify patterns without overstating incident-reporting data.
  5. Track action and effectiveness through governance.
