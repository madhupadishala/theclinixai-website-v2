Pharmacovigilance Training for Hospital Staff

Training should match the decisions each role makes: recognising suspected reactions, documenting evidence, responding clinically, preserving source information, reporting promptly and participating in learning. Completion records alone do not demonstrate competence.

01

Role relevance

Tailor examples and responsibilities to doctors, pharmacists, nurses, quality and leadership.

02

Scenario practice

Use cases involving ADRs, medication errors, special situations and incomplete information.

03

Competency evidence

Assess application, escalation and documentation rather than attendance alone.

CONTROL FRAMEWORK

Recognise. Report. Review. Learn. Prevent.

  1. Make reporting accessible to relevant staff and patients.
  2. Preserve clinical evidence and protect information appropriately.
  3. Apply accountable assessment and escalation.
  4. Identify patterns without overstating incident-reporting data.
  5. Track action and effectiveness through governance.

AUTHORISED CONTEXT
