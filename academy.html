FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions a serious applicant should ask before enrolling.

Is TheClinixAI Academy a course or a traineeship?

The Academy includes different routes. The paid individual routes are structured as practical development and traineeship-style programmes using controlled internal learning scenarios, guided review, documented assignments and competency assessment. The exact terms applicable to you are stated in your written onboarding documents.

Why is there a fee for the paid route?

The fee supports structured training delivery, instructor and reviewer time, assessment administration, controlled learning materials, platform access and programme operations. It is not a payment for a job, an experience letter or guaranteed placement.

Does paying ₹19,999 guarantee a job or placement?

No. TheClinixAI Academy does not sell jobs or guarantee placement. Any future role depends on demonstrated competency, professional conduct, business need, open positions and the applicable selection process.

Will I work on live client cases?

No live client casework is promised as part of Academy enrolment. Practical work uses controlled internal, simulated, blinded or otherwise approved learning scenarios. Any future access to confidential material would require separate authorisation, role need and confidentiality controls.

What exactly is PV Nexus exposure?

PV Nexus is TheClinixAI's pharmacovigilance technology environment. Selected Academy activities use controlled training workflows to teach structured intake, literature review, evidence linking, coding logic, quality feedback, audit trails and responsible human-in-the-loop use of automation.

How will my performance be assessed?

Depending on the route, assessment can include knowledge checks, controlled assignments, case-based exercises, quality feedback, correction cycles and defined readiness expectations. Progression is based on demonstrated capability rather than attendance alone.

Will I receive an internship, traineeship or experience document?

Documentation depends on your selected route, successful completion, actual tenure and the written programme terms. Any issued document should accurately describe the engagement completed and must not be used to misrepresent permanent employment.

How does background verification work?

Where you are formally onboarded under an applicable arrangement and records are maintained, legitimate future verification requests can be answered according to those records, including the actual tenure and designation stated in the formal documents.

Is this suitable for someone with no pharmacovigilance experience?

Yes. The fresher routes begin with foundational PV and compliance concepts. However, applicants are screened because the programme expects analytical thinking, professional ownership, remote-work discipline and willingness to accept and act on feedback.

Can I join only to obtain a certificate?

If your primary goal is simply to collect a certificate, this programme may not be the right fit. The Academy is designed around practical work, review, revision and evidence of capability.

What should I rely on if marketing copy and my offer terms differ?

Your formal written offer, onboarding terms and programme policy govern your engagement. Website content explains the model but does not override the documents issued specifically to you.

Candidate protection: Ask questions before you pay. The Academy team should be able to explain the route, fee, schedule, assessment model, documentation and limitations applicable to your intake.
