WHAT YOU LEARN

A complete view of the PV workflow.

The curriculum is intentionally broader than a basic “ICSR + MedDRA” course. Topics are connected to the work they support and the decisions they influence.

01 · FOUNDATION

Pharmacovigilance foundations

Safety concepts, PV lifecycle, stakeholders, terminology, reporting obligations and operational context.

Build the vocabulary and workflow map.
02 · ICSR

Case processing

Intake, triage, validity, duplicates, seriousness, expectedness, causality, follow-up and case closure.

Understand the case from source to outcome.
03 · MEDDRA

Medical coding

Hierarchy, LLT, PT, HLT, HLGT, SOC and practical adverse-event and indication coding concepts.

Code with medical context.
04 · WRITING

Narrative development

Chronology, clinical coherence, source evidence, relevant facts, follow-up and quality expectations.

Write a defensible safety narrative.
05 · QUALITY

QC and review

Common case-quality errors, review principles, consistency checks, feedback and readiness for approval.

Learn why quality gates exist.
06 · LITERATURE

Literature surveillance

Search, screening, relevance assessment, full-text review, literature cases and evidence traceability.

Connect literature to PV action.
07 · SIGNALS

Signal management

Signal concepts, detection, validation, evaluation, case series and risk-focused follow-up.

Understand the signal lifecycle.
08 · AGGREGATE

Aggregate safety

PSUR/PBRER, PADER, DSUR, benefit-risk concepts and the role of aggregate evidence.

See beyond individual cases.
09 · REGULATORY

Regulatory PV

Global safety reporting concepts, timelines, GVP, ICH and regional regulatory expectations.

Understand why requirements differ.
10 · CLINICAL

Clinical-trial safety

SAE, SUSAR, expedited reporting, clinical safety workflows and the DSUR context.

Connect PV to clinical development.
11 · MEDICAL

Medical review

Clinical reasoning, case assessment, causality, medical relevance and safety decision support.

Develop evidence-led judgement.
12 · TECHNOLOGY

AI-enabled PV

Governed automation, human-in-the-loop review, evidence traceability and responsible AI in PV workflows.

Understand modern PV operating models.

PRACTICAL EXPOSURE

What practical learning actually means here.

Not just a list of modules

You are expected to connect concepts to workflow: why a case is valid, why seriousness matters, how coding affects downstream safety data, why narratives need chronology and how quality review changes the final output.

Controlled, not careless

Client confidentiality is not used as a marketing promise. Practical learning uses approved training scenarios, simulations, blinded or controlled material as applicable. Any confidential access requires separate authorization.

Technology-aware

Selected activities can introduce the operating logic of modern PV technology, evidence traceability, structured data and governed AI workflows.

Quality is part of learning

Review is not an afterthought. Learners need to understand why a safety decision or case output is accepted, rejected, corrected or escalated.

CAREER READINESS

From “I completed a course” to “I understand the work.”

No programme can honestly guarantee a job. What it can do is help you build stronger foundations, practical understanding, better interview answers and evidence of how you approach PV work.

What theory-only learning often leaves behind

Knowing definitions without being able to explain the sequence of a case, justify a decision, identify an error, write a coherent narrative or discuss how quality controls affect PV operations.

What we aim to develop

Workflow understanding, structured reasoning, medical and regulatory context, quality awareness, documentation discipline, feedback responsiveness and the confidence to discuss practical PV scenarios.

ACADEMY FAQ

Questions worth answering before you apply.

What is TheClinixAI Pharmacovigilance Apprenticeship?

It is a structured practical development programme designed to help suitable learners understand and practise Pharmacovigilance workflows through guided learning, assignments, review and competency-focused progression.

Is the apprenticeship suitable for freshers?

Yes. Suitable graduates, postgraduates, Pharm.D candidates and other life-science professionals can apply. Selection depends on programme requirements and readiness for structured learning and feedback.

What will I learn?

Coverage can include Pharmacovigilance foundations, ICSR processing, case intake and triage, seriousness, expectedness, causality, MedDRA, narrative writing, quality review, literature surveillance, signal management, aggregate safety reporting, regulatory concepts, medical review and AI-enabled PV workflows.

Is the free internship part of the Academy?

No. The Academy is positioned around the apprenticeship. Internship opportunities, when available, are handled separately through TheClinixAI Careers.

Does the apprenticeship guarantee a job?

No. TheClinixAI does not sell jobs or guarantee employment. Career outcomes depend on demonstrated capability, vacancies, employer requirements and applicable selection processes.

Will I work on confidential client cases?

Client case access is not promised. Practical learning uses approved training scenarios, simulations, blinded or controlled material as applicable. Any confidential access requires separate authorization and appropriate controls.
