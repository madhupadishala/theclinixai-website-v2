THE APPRENTICESHIP

Built for people who want to become useful in PV.

TheClinixAI Apprenticeship is our primary individual Academy route. It is designed around structured learning, practical exercises, guided review and competency development.

We do not believe that completing a list of modules automatically makes someone ready for a Pharmacovigilance role. The programme therefore connects concepts to workflows and workflows to decisions.

Work through controlled case-based and workflow exercises.

Build career readiness without promising a job as the product.
WHAT YOU LEARN

More than ICSR and MedDRA.

Students searching for Pharmacovigilance training are often shown a list of topics. We go one level deeper: what the topic means, where it appears in the workflow, why it matters and what professional judgement is expected.

01 · FOUNDATION

Pharmacovigilance fundamentals

PV purpose, safety lifecycle, stakeholders, sources of safety information and core terminology.

Concept → workflow → responsibility
02 · ICSR

Individual Case Safety Reports

Intake, triage, validity, duplicates, seriousness, expectedness, causality, follow-up and case closure.

Understand the end-to-end case journey
03 · MEDDRA

MedDRA & medical coding

Hierarchy, terminology selection, coding logic and practical thinking around consistent medical terminology.

LLT · PT · HLT · HLGT · SOC
04 · NARRATIVE

Safety narrative writing

How to turn case information into a clear, chronological and clinically meaningful narrative.

Accuracy · chronology · completeness
05 · QUALITY

QC & review mindset

Identify omissions, inconsistencies, documentation gaps and quality issues before work moves forward.

Review → correct → learn
06 · LITERATURE

Literature surveillance

Search, screening, relevance assessment, full-text thinking and the relationship between literature and ICSR processing.

Evidence → screening → decision
07 · SIGNAL

Signal detection & management

Safety signals, validation, assessment, case series, trends and the role of structured evidence in safety decisions.

From observation to safety assessment
08 · AGGREGATE

Aggregate safety reporting

PSUR, PBRER, PADER, DSUR concepts, periodic review and benefit-risk thinking.

Periodic evidence → safety narrative
09 · REGULATORY

Regulatory Pharmacovigilance

Core reporting concepts, timelines, GVP/ICH/FDA/EMA/CDSCO context and compliance thinking.

Know the framework behind the workflow
10 · CLINICAL

Clinical-trial safety

SAE, SUSAR, clinical-trial ICSRs, expedited reporting and DSUR context.

Clinical safety vs post-marketing PV
11 · MEDICAL

Medical review & assessment

Clinical assessment, causality reasoning, case quality and the relationship between medical judgement and PV operations.

Evidence before opinion
12 · AI + PV

Modern AI-enabled PV

Human-in-the-loop automation, evidence traceability, review controls and where AI can responsibly support PV work.

Automation with expert oversight

ACADEMY QUESTIONS

Before you apply.

Is TheClinixAI Academy a course or an apprenticeship?

The primary individual offering is a structured Pharmacovigilance Apprenticeship. It is built around practical development, guided learning, assignments, review and demonstrated competency rather than certificate-only learning.

Is it suitable for freshers?

Yes. Suitable graduates, postgraduates, Pharm.D candidates and other life-science professionals can apply. The programme expects professional ownership, analytical thinking, willingness to receive feedback and consistent participation.

What will I learn?

Coverage can include PV fundamentals, ICSR processing, case intake and triage, seriousness, expectedness, causality, MedDRA, narrative writing, QC, literature surveillance, signal management, aggregate safety, regulatory PV, clinical-trial safety, medical review and AI-enabled PV workflows.

Does the programme guarantee a job?

No. TheClinixAI does not sell jobs or guarantee employment. Career outcomes depend on demonstrated capability, selection processes, vacancies, experience, conduct and employer requirements.

Where is the free internship?

The free internship is intentionally not part of the Academy positioning. Internship opportunities, when available, are handled separately through TheClinixAI Careers channel so that the Academy remains focused on the apprenticeship.

Will I work on confidential client cases?

Client case access is not promised. Practical learning uses approved training scenarios, simulations, blinded or controlled material as applicable. Any confidential access requires separate authorization and appropriate controls.
