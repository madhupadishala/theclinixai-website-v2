# Earned authority and backlink activation

Backlinks are counted only after an independent website publishes a crawlable reference. A submitted profile, drafted guest article or promised mention is not recorded as a completed backlink.

## Priority assets

1. Original ClinixAI research papers.
2. Source-led PV mother guides.
3. Responsible-AI governance framework.
4. Hospital medication-safety guidance.
5. Company fact sheet and expert-commentary page.

## Legitimate acquisition routes

- Contribute expert commentary to pharmaceutical, clinical-research and healthcare-technology publications.
- Present original research or operational frameworks through professional associations and conferences.
- Collaborate with hospitals, institutions and academic departments on educational or research material.
- Maintain accurate company profiles on relevant business and technology databases.
- Publish partner announcements only after written approval from all named parties.
- Offer source-led guest articles where editorial ownership and disclosure rules are clear.

## Quality gate

Before recording a backlink, confirm:

- The referring page is publicly crawlable.
- The mention is relevant and factually accurate.
- The anchor text is natural.
- The link is not purchased, hidden or part of an automated link scheme.
- The page is not a copied press-release farm.
- The evidence URL and publication date are recorded.

Use `press/earned-authority-tracker.csv` to govern targets and verified results.
