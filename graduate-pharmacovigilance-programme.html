Graduate Pharmacovigilance ProgrammeA practical pharmacovigilance development pathway for healthcare graduates seeking structured exposure to safety operations, scientific judgement and workplace expectations.

Healthcare graduates often know the definitions but have not yet applied them under a controlled workflow. This programme focuses on the transition from remembering PV concepts to evaluating evidence and documenting a defensible decision.

01

Decision practice

Participants evaluate patient, reporter, product, event, seriousness and follow-up scenarios.

02

Workflow understanding

Learning follows the safety lifecycle rather than disconnected classroom topics.

03

Professional readiness

Feedback focuses on accuracy, rationale, communication, ownership and controlled execution.
