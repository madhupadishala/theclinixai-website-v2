PHARMACOVIGILANCE · APPRENTICESHIP

Build the PV experience you need to work as a Pharmacovigilance Associate.

As a Drug Safety Associate Apprentice, you will gain expertise in PV basics, regulatory and GVP foundations and SOP-led working. You will get hands-on experience with safety workflows, approved safety databases and PV Nexus where applicable, with continuous feedback from the QC team.

Explore Drug Safety Associate Apprentice
As a Drug Safety Associate Apprentice, you will gain:
  • PV fundamentals and regulatory awareness
  • GVP and SOP working discipline
  • Hands-on safety case workflow experience
  • Exposure to real-world safety databases where authorized
  • Continuous QC review and feedback
  • Progression toward individual contributor readiness

What opportunities does TheClinixAI offer?

How do I apply?

No. The objective is to build practical capability and experience. Any future employment depends on demonstrated readiness, business need, available positions and the applicable selection process.
