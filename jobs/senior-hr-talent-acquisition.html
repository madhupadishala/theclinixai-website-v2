Senior HR & Talent Acquisition Specialist

Hyderabad / Remote · 3–5 years experience · Human Resources

About the role

Own end-to-end talent acquisition while supporting core HR operations and employee experience. You will partner closely with leadership and functional managers to build high-quality teams across pharmacovigilance, technology and corporate functions.

Key responsibilities

  • Own end-to-end recruitment from workforce requirement intake through sourcing, screening, interview coordination, selection and offer closure.
  • Build talent pipelines for PV, technology, HR, finance, marketing and leadership roles.
  • Use LinkedIn, job portals, professional communities, referrals and targeted sourcing to identify candidates.
  • Develop structured screening criteria, interview scorecards and candidate communication workflows.
  • Coordinate with hiring managers to define role requirements, interview plans and selection decisions.
  • Manage offer discussions, joining follow-up and pre-boarding.
  • Maintain accurate recruitment trackers, hiring metrics and candidate records.
  • Support onboarding, employee documentation, HR policies, attendance and employee lifecycle processes.
  • Maintain confidentiality and ensure fair, consistent and compliant hiring practices.
  • Help strengthen employer branding and candidate experience.

Required qualifications

  • Bachelor's or master's degree in HR, Business Administration or related field.
  • 3–5 years of hands-on recruitment and HR experience, preferably in healthcare, life sciences, technology or professional services.
  • Strong sourcing and stakeholder-management capability.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication.
  • Ability to manage multiple open roles and priorities independently.

Preferred skills

  • Experience hiring pharmacovigilance and software/technology professionals.
  • Strong LinkedIn sourcing and Boolean search capability.
  • Experience with HRIS/ATS systems and recruitment analytics.
  • Working knowledge of Indian employment documentation and HR compliance.
