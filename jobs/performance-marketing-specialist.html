MARKETING · FULL-TIME

Performance Marketing Specialist

Hyderabad / Remote · 2–4 years experience · Digital Marketing

About the role

Own measurable digital growth across paid acquisition, conversion and campaign optimization for TheClinixAI's healthcare technology and professional audiences. This is a hands-on performance role combining campaign execution, analytics, creative testing and landing-page optimization.

Key responsibilities

  • Plan, launch and optimize paid campaigns across Google Ads, Meta Ads and other relevant channels.
  • Own campaign setup, audience targeting, budgets, bidding, tracking, UTM governance and conversion measurement.
  • Monitor CPL, CPA, ROAS, CTR, CVR, CAC and funnel performance and turn insights into actions.
  • Build and test ad creative, messaging, landing pages and audience segments to improve conversion.
  • Coordinate with content, design, web and sales teams to align campaigns with business objectives.
  • Implement and validate GA4, Google Tag Manager, pixels and conversion events.
  • Prepare weekly and monthly performance reports with clear recommendations.
  • Conduct competitor, keyword, audience and channel research.
  • Support SEO and organic growth initiatives where relevant, while keeping paid and organic strategy aligned.

Required qualifications

  • 2–4 years of hands-on performance marketing experience.
  • Demonstrated experience managing paid acquisition campaigns and budgets.
  • Strong Google Ads, Meta Ads and GA4 knowledge.
  • Strong analytical ability and comfort working with campaign and funnel data.
  • Excellent copy sense and understanding of conversion-focused creative.

Preferred skills

  • Google Ads certifications, Looker Studio, GTM and basic SQL.
  • Experience marketing B2B SaaS, healthcare, life sciences or professional services.
  • Experience with CRM funnel attribution and lead-quality analysis.
