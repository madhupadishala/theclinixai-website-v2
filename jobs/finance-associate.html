Finance Associate

Hyderabad / Remote · 2+ years experience · Finance

About the role

Support accurate, timely finance operations across accounting, invoicing, reconciliations, reporting and business administration. The role requires strong ownership of records, attention to detail and the ability to work closely with HR, operations and leadership.

Key responsibilities

  • Maintain accounting records, vouchers, invoices and supporting documentation.
  • Support accounts payable and receivable activities and track outstanding items.
  • Perform bank, ledger and balance-sheet reconciliations and investigate discrepancies.
  • Prepare periodic finance reports, expense summaries and management information.
  • Support payroll inputs and coordination with HR and external finance partners as applicable.
  • Assist with GST, TDS, statutory filings and documentation in coordination with accountants or advisors.
  • Track receivables, payables, expenses and cash-flow requirements.
  • Maintain organized digital finance records and support audit requirements.
  • Help improve finance processes, controls and reporting accuracy.

Required qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Commerce, Finance, Accounting or related discipline.
  • 2+ years of relevant finance/accounting experience.
  • Strong Excel skills and practical understanding of accounting processes.
  • Good understanding of invoices, reconciliations, receivables, payables and statutory basics.
  • High attention to accuracy, confidentiality and deadlines.

Preferred skills

  • Experience with Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks or another accounting platform.
  • Working knowledge of Indian GST/TDS processes.
  • Experience in a growing technology or services company.
