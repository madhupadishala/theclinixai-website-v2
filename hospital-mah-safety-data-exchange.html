Hospital–MAH Safety-Data Exchange

Hospitals and MAHs may need to exchange suspected adverse-reaction information while respecting applicable privacy, ethics, consent, contractual and regulatory requirements. Responsibilities and follow-up routes should be clear before urgent cases arise.

01

Defined scope

Clarify products, report types, parties, contacts, evidence and permitted data exchange.

02

Controlled transfer

Preserve source, date, case reference, acknowledgement and follow-up status.

03

Reconciliation

Identify missing transfers, mismatched records, duplicates and outstanding information.

CONTROL FRAMEWORK

Recognise. Report. Review. Learn. Prevent.

  1. Make reporting accessible to relevant staff and patients.
  2. Preserve clinical evidence and protect information appropriately.
  3. Apply accountable assessment and escalation.
  4. Identify patterns without overstating incident-reporting data.
  5. Track action and effectiveness through governance.
