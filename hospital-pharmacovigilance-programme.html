Hospital Pharmacovigilance Programme

Hospitals observe suspected adverse reactions, medication errors, treatment failures and special situations at the point of care. A dependable programme makes reporting simple, preserves clinical evidence and turns individual reports into organisational learning.

01

Governance

Define leadership, committee oversight, reporting routes, responsibilities and escalation.

02

Clinical reporting

Enable doctors, nurses, pharmacists and patients to report relevant medicine-safety information.

03

Learning

Review trends, contributing factors, outcomes and actions without reducing the programme to report counts.

CONTROL FRAMEWORK

Recognise. Report. Review. Learn. Prevent.

  1. Make reporting accessible to relevant staff and patients.
  2. Preserve clinical evidence and protect information appropriately.
  3. Apply accountable assessment and escalation.
  4. Identify patterns without overstating incident-reporting data.
  5. Track action and effectiveness through governance.
