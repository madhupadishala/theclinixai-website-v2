1. What this policy covers

This cookie policy explains the cookies, browser storage and related technologies used on theclinixai.com. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

Some storage is essential for the website to operate securely or remember a privacy choice. Optional analytics remains off unless you accept it.

2. The categories we use

Essential storage

This supports security, site operation and the storage of your privacy choice. It cannot be disabled through our banner because the choice itself must be remembered.

Analytics storage

This helps us understand visits, content engagement and enquiry journeys. It is optional and is activated only after you select “Accept analytics” or enable analytics in Cookie settings.

Advertising storage

TheClinixAI does not enable advertising or personalised-advertising storage through this website consent configuration.

4. Google Analytics 4

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages and resources are useful and how visitors navigate the site. Our configuration denies advertising storage, ad-user-data use and ad personalisation. Google Analytics is not loaded until analytics is accepted.

We configure our measurement so form contents are not intentionally sent to Google Analytics. For more information about how Google handles information, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. How to control or withdraw your choice

Use the control to accept, reject or change optional analytics at any time. Rejecting optional analytics prevents new ClinixAI analytics events and keeps Google Analytics from loading on later page visits.

You can also delete cookies and site storage through your browser settings. Doing so may remove the saved privacy choice, causing the banner to appear again.

Browser privacy controls may affect how technologies behave. Blocking all browser storage can also affect necessary website functions.

6. Updates

We may update this policy when technologies, providers or regulatory requirements change. The effective and review dates above identify the current version.
