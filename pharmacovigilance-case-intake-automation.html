THE OPERATIONAL RISK

Day zero is often lost before case processing begins.

Reports can enter through medical information, quality, sales, partners, literature, websites and patient programmes. When intake is fragmented across inboxes and spreadsheets, awareness dates become contestable and source evidence can be separated from the case. Nexus Safety Intake creates a governed front door for safety information without confusing intake with final case assessment.

CONTROLLED END-TO-END FLOW

One workflow. Five accountable decisions.

01

Receive

Capture structured and unstructured safety information from configured organisational channels.

02

Preserve

Retain the original source, receipt timestamp, attachments and routing history.

03

Identify

Propose minimum-criteria evidence and flag missing or ambiguous information.

04

Triage

Prioritise seriousness, special situations, deadlines and potential duplicates for review.

05

Route

Create a controlled hand-off to case processing with no loss of provenance.

QUESTIONS SAFETY LEADERS ASK

Frequently asked questions

What does pharmacovigilance case intake automation cover?

It covers controlled receipt, source preservation, awareness-date evidence, preliminary minimum-criteria identification, triage and routing into the case workflow.

Is intake the same as ICSR case processing?

No. Intake establishes and preserves incoming safety information. Case processing performs the governed case assessment, coding, narrative, medical review, QC and submission preparation.

Can a non-safety employee trigger day zero?

Regional rules and company procedures govern awareness, but safety information received by relevant parts of an organisation can affect regulatory timelines. Routing controls therefore matter.

Can AI declare a report valid automatically?

ClinixAI can identify evidence and flag gaps, but authorised users confirm validity and regulatory consequences.
