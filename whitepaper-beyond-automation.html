Beyond Automation

The next generation of pharmacovigilance literature intelligence.

AB
Adithya BClinixAI Research • 18 min read • Version 1.0

Automation alone does not make literature surveillance intelligent. The real challenge is preserving evidence, regulatory context and human accountability while reducing repetitive work.

Evidence before automation

A trustworthy workflow begins with traceable source material. Every extracted fact, decision and reviewer action should remain connected to the publication and the rule that shaped it.

Human judgement stays central

AI can prioritise, retrieve and explain. Medical and regulatory decisions remain with qualified safety professionals.
